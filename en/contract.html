Rental Agreements and Subletting

Renewals, adding a housemate and subletting
🔍
Q1My rental agreement is expiring — can I renew it?›

Generally, if the landlord has no other plans, and you have lived in the property responsibly, caused no damage and kept it clean and tidy, you can renew for one year or half a year. Ask the PM about the specific arrangements.

Q2I have a new housemate — how do I add them to the rental agreement?›

Yes. Send your housemate's details to your Property Manager for assessment: passport or driver's licence; visa and Confirmation of Enrolment (COE), or Medicare card; bank statement or payslip; and a completed application form. Once the application is approved, we will prepare a new rental agreement. Any related fee will be limited to the reasonable actual cost and confirmed in writing.

Q3I cannot continue my rental agreement due to circumstances beyond my control — what should I do?›

You may find a replacement renter to take over the rental agreement. A transfer fee may apply, as specified in your rental agreement. Once a replacement renter has been confirmed, the fee may be paid separately or deducted from the bond after you move out.

You may advertise on online social media yourself, or ask us to advertise the property through channels such as realestate.com and Domain. The advertising fee is a one-off payment of $330, payable in advance to O REAL (BSB 733003, Acc 727393).

Please note that paying the advertising fee does not guarantee that we will find a replacement renter. The outgoing renter remains responsible for rent and any related costs until the new rental agreement begins or the existing rental agreement otherwise legally ends.

Q4I have found someone to take over the rental agreement — what happens next?›

Send the proposed replacement renter's details to your Property Manager for assessment: passport or driver's licence; visa and Confirmation of Enrolment (COE), or Medicare card; bank statement or payslip; and a completed application form. Once the application is approved, we will prepare a new rental agreement. After the replacement renter has signed the agreement and paid the bond and first month's rent, you can pay the transfer fee and arrange the move-out process.
